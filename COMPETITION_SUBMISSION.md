# Competition submission

## Design rationale

Most players let you watch and seek. This skin is for the moments in between: wandering through a film by scene without losing your place, and keeping the bits you want to come back to. A wheel of films and scenes sits beside the picture, and jumping to a scene leaves a Return Anchor so one press takes you back. A personal atlas collects the moments you save, per film, in your own browser with no account.

The look is a dark instrument: obsidian surfaces, one citron accent for what is active, and lavender for memory. It is meant to stay out of the way over bright and dark footage alike.
